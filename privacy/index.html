3. The SystemBoy app

SystemBoy runs on your own computer. There is no user account, no advertising and no tracking of your content. We receive no conversation content, files or information about your computer – except for the anonymous usage statistics.

Anonymous usage statistics

So that we know how many SystemBoy installations are actively used and which versions and systems are common, the app sends a short message to our server api.systemboy.app (hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Gunzenhausen, Germany, in a data centre in the EU) at start-up and at most once a day. It contains:

  • a randomly generated installation ID (no hardware, device or personal identifier; it is created randomly at installation and stored locally),
  • the SystemBoy version, the operating system (Windows, macOS or Linux), the processor architecture and the selected language.

The time of the first and last message and the number of starts are also stored. IP addresses are not stored or logged; the content of your conversations, files, user or computer names are never transmitted. We cannot identify you.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in developing, planning and securing SystemBoy (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); storing the random ID on your device is required for this (Section 25(2) TDDDG). Entries that have not received a message for 12 months are deleted.

Objection: You can object to the usage statistics at any time (Art. 21 GDPR) – in the app under “Settings › Privacy › Anonymous usage statistics”. SystemBoy then stops sending messages and your entry on our server is deleted.

Local storage

Settings (e.g. language, experience level, allowed folders), conversation history and sign-in data for AI services are stored exclusively locally in the app's data folder on your computer. You can remove them by uninstalling and deleting the data folder.

AI services (transfer to third parties)

So that SystemBoy can help, your messages and the information SystemBoy reads on your computer (e.g. system information, error messages, program lists and – only with your approval – file contents) are sent to and processed by the AI provider you choose. That provider is responsible for this processing under its own terms, for example:

  • Free AI (default): free models from OpenCode, operated by Anomaly Innovations, Inc. (USA), with servers in the USA and the EU. Their terms of service and privacy policy apply. SystemBoy only offers free models whose inputs, according to OpenCode, are not used for training; depending on the model, data may be retained for up to 30 days for safety reasons. For use without a paid account, OpenCode reserves the right to use content to improve its services.
  • Web login or API key: e.g. OpenAI (ChatGPT), GitHub (Copilot), xAI (Grok), Google (Gemini) or another provider you choose. Its privacy terms apply.
  • Local AI (Ollama, LM Studio): data does not leave your computer.

Please do not enter passwords, PINs, bank details or other highly sensitive data in the chat. Using an AI service is your decision (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR or your own responsibility as a private user).

Protected folders

SystemBoy only accesses typical private folders (Desktop, Documents, Pictures, Videos, Music, Downloads, OneDrive, iCloud Drive, Mail, Messages) with your approval. You can change this setting in the app at any time.

Version check

SystemBoy regularly (at start and about every 6 hours) retrieves the status file status.json from this website or from GitHub to check whether the installed version is still supported. Only technically necessary data such as your IP address is transmitted to GitHub; no identifier, user name or license is sent. An entered license is checked and stored locally only. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR (performance of contract or legitimate interest in enforcing the terms of use).

Data stored locally: SystemBoy stores chat history, device profile, notes, measurements, change log, backup copies of changed files, reports and the knowledge base only on your computer. You can delete them in the app at any time; the retention period of the chat history is configurable. These data are only sent to the AI provider when needed for your question (e.g. device profile and notes as context; can be turned off under “My PC”).

Speed test: Only when you start a measurement under “My PC › Network” does SystemBoy download and upload test data from speed.cloudflare.com (Cloudflare, Inc.). Your IP address is transmitted to Cloudflare in the process. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(a) or (b) GDPR (function triggered by you).

Updates

SystemBoy regularly checks whether new versions are available. Requests are sent to GitHub (github.com, for app updates) and to the npm registry (registry.npmjs.org, operated by GitHub/npm, Inc., for updates of the bundled AI agent OpenCode). Technically necessary data such as your IP address and app version are transmitted. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure, up-to-date software).

Local AI detection

On start, SystemBoy checks whether Ollama or LM Studio is running on your computer (request only to your own machine, 127.0.0.1).
