Security & privacy

An agent with access to your PC needs clear limits.

That's why security at SystemBoy isn't an add-on but the foundation: you approve every change, private folders stay locked and your data stays on your computer.

Six firm ground rules

Nothing without your consent

Every command and every file change is shown and explained first. Dangerous commands are flagged.

Private folders stay private

Documents, Pictures, Downloads, Desktop, OneDrive, iCloud, Mail and Messages are locked by default – access only after approval.

Undo built in

Originals are backed up before every file change. One-click restore point before risky operations.

No SystemBoy cloud for your content

No account. Your messages, files and conversations never go to SystemBoy – only to the AI service you choose; with local AI everything stays on your computer. SystemBoy only receives the anonymous usage statistics (can be switched off).

No tracking, no ads

No analytics, no advertising IDs, no hidden data collection. History and profile stay local and can be deleted any time.

Honest about limits

AI can be wrong. SystemBoy tells you upfront – and replaces neither antivirus nor a professional for hardware damage.

Only genuine, approved updates

SystemBoy updates itself automatically – but only with versions the publisher has explicitly approved.

Signed approval

Every new version comes with an approval signed with the publisher key (Ed25519). If it is missing or doesn't match, SystemBoy downloads and installs nothing.

Checksum before installing

Every update file is checked against the approval with SHA-512 – after downloading and once more before installing. A modified file is discarded.

The installer checks the same way

The SystemBoy installer for Windows and macOS uses the same check: it only installs the latest version approved by the publisher.

To be honest: the program files themselves are not yet signed with a paid certificate (Microsoft Authenticode, Apple notarization). That's why Windows and macOS show a notice on first start. How to start SystemBoy for the first time

Where does my data go?

Your computer

Profile, history, measurements, backups, knowledge base – all local.

Only what's needed

Information needed for your question goes encrypted to …

Your AI service

… the provider of your choice – or a local AI without internet.

Which AI service gets what?

Your messages and the information needed for your question only go to the AI service you choose. Its own terms apply to that processing.

Free AI (default)

Free models from OpenCode (Anomaly Innovations, USA). SystemBoy only offers models whose inputs, according to OpenCode, are not used for training; depending on the model retained for up to 30 days. Without a paid account, OpenCode reserves the right to use content to improve its services.

Your own account or API key

ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Claude, Gemini and many more: the privacy terms of the provider you connect apply.

Local AI

With Ollama or LM Studio the AI runs on your computer – your data doesn't leave it.

Anonymous usage statistics

At most once a day: random installation ID, version, system and language – no IP addresses, no content. Can be turned off under Settings › Privacy.

Don't enter passwords, PINs or bank details in the chat. All details in the privacy policy
