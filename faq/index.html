FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Cost, AI providers, privacy and installation – answered briefly.

Is SystemBoy really free?

Yes. SystemBoy is free for private use. The built-in AI access has a daily limit; with your own account or a local AI there is none.

How is SystemBoy different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT can't see your computer. SystemBoy reads real system data, knows your device, finds the cause and can fix it itself – with your consent and an undo function.

Can I use ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?

Yes. ChatGPT via browser sign-in with your Plus or Pro plan, Claude, Gemini and over 300 other providers with your own API key. A Claude Pro subscription can't be used.

Can SystemBoy damage my computer?

Every change needs your approval, files are backed up first and a restore point is one click away. Still, any system change can have side effects – check suggestions before approving.

Does SystemBoy read my private files?

No, not without your permission. Documents, Pictures, Downloads, Desktop, cloud folders, Mail and Messages are locked until you allow access.

What data is transmitted?

Only what your question needs, and only to the AI service you chose. History, profile, measurements and knowledge base stay on your computer. With a local AI nothing leaves your machine.

Does SystemBoy work offline?

With a local AI (Ollama or LM Studio), yes. The tools under “My PC” run locally anyway.

Does SystemBoy run in the background?

Only if you enable background mode. Then it checks storage, drives, battery, temperature and crashes every 15 minutes and lets you know about problems.

Does SystemBoy replace my antivirus?

No. The security score checks whether antivirus, firewall, encryption and backup are set up correctly – it doesn't replace a virus scanner.

Does SystemBoy replace an IT technician?

For many everyday problems, yes. For hardware defects or complex faults, the support package helps your technician understand what's going on faster.

Why does Windows warn me during installation?

SystemBoy isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. The warning is normal for new programs; continue via “More info” › “Run anyway”.

Is SystemBoy available in German?

Yes – app, website and installer are available in English and German. SystemBoy also replies in other languages if you write in them.

What does the SystemBoy installer do?

On Windows and macOS it always downloads the latest version from the official release, checks the publisher signature and the SHA-512 checksum and only then installs SystemBoy. The direct packages remain available on the download page.

How does SystemBoy update itself?

Automatically. SystemBoy only installs versions the publisher has approved with a signature and whose checksum matches – a modified file is discarded.
