Typical problems – and what SystemBoy does

Printer won't print

Checks queue, service and driver, clears stuck jobs, reconnects.

Internet is slow

Measures step by step: Wi-Fi, router, DNS, packet loss – and shows where it stalls.

PC is slow

Finds resource hogs, startup clutter, memory pressure, thermal throttling and full drives.

Disk full

Maps folders, finds large files and duplicates. Deletes only what you approve.

Checks audio service, default device, volume and driver – and sets it right.

Crashes & blue screens

Reads crash reports and error codes, narrows down driver, hardware or app.
